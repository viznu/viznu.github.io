of a sullen day
beat down on

wedged

TETRAPOD crabs heralding another sunset
to fastfood romance,
reedy lower middle-class bones,phones,hopes and breaths,
the OOze,the converge,
trinket pimps,godmen and encashed eunuch blessings,
the odorific chutzpah of this \#culturAyahuasca !!

fisherwomen marooned
in human deluge,
religions come in colours and
otherwise;vermillion,Aum,saffron,quaaludes,white,china white.

salty megalopilitan breeze,in night's dry husk.
The monsoons are a-comin, my love.
