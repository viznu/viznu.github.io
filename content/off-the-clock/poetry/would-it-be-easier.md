<div class="poem">

would it be easier
knowing that you are right
when it would mean nothing
at all to the rest of us
ghosts slinking down the stairs of time

the walls bleed
with the rains
our storms weep
but it would be nicer
if you let it all go

waiting in vain
for a sign
when all i needed
was to strive

under what
newspaper skies
and
television floors
do i dream
such lowly dreams

</div>
