<div class="poem">

My bluest despairs
Arrange my pain
Bones and all
broken
on my own altar
Lay I

By dimlit paths amidst
Old hills,
Starlit by promises
my sweet boy
How I miss your tiny frame

This lonesome song
Of my love for you,
Will brook no chance listener,
In this valley of time.

</div>
