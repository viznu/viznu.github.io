<div class="poem">

There by them
Dreaming hills
Where the concrete
boughs cease
Amidst twisted oak
and wrung out pine

We sat and gazed on
Yesterday's tombstones
Perched atop breezy
surf of green,
And swimming yellow
Poppies
Lonely cars
The silences gilded
With quiet words

</div>
