<div class="poem">

I said: I feel like we've known each other in other lives.
You said: I'm certain of it.
I said: Maybe you were the cow and I was the grass.
You said: I like that.
—- And smiled with your eyes. —-

I said: I'm pretty sure you were older than me in other lives too.
You laughed: Oh really?
I said: Yes. You already act that way now. Wizened by sadness.
You said: Childish curiosity makes you seem younger.
—-We laughed. ——
I said: That sounds like an insult wrapped in a compliment.

I said: Do you think this is the lifetime where our paths will stay crossed? And for how long?
You said: nothing.
