<div class="poem">

I will walk unfettered
In the halls of light
In the gardens of my mind.
By the curving beach
the sands of
All the salt
Of the sea of sorrow.
Waiting untethered
At the lights edge.
The deed for its own sake
I bleed words with a pained grace
Smile at the cold vastness of the void,
Come ravage as you might,
Here I stand,
To violent ends I will tread,
To dream the light
Where none will shine.

</div>
