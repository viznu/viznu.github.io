<div class="poem">

outta the sun-blasted hills of
Mumbai mahanagari
Architected of colonized despair
And cursed resilience
To the monazite shores
By the southern sands
Where dreams sprung east into and through weeping green hills
And west into the sea
into the  sea
into the sea
I venture
Again and again and again

Old hills - pillars of haunted time

</div>
