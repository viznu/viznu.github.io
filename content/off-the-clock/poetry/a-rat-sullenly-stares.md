<div class="poem">

a rat sullenly stares at the grey dawn sky, it's crow-teased innards making up for all the red for that morning.

Sometimes a man can feel drowned by an ocean of thought, an unbiased deluge of feeling, hopelessly lost under the vastness of the water's dark blue roof above him. And yet a moment of stillness would beckon, to struggle no more and be.

An exacting moment of stillness when the world's edges become more acute and colour more defined and sound hurtful. Teetering at the edge of that ravening drop into the infinity of a moment, is like walking a tightrope across insanity's pits.

</div>
