<div class="poem">

Even lonesome
A single shaft of burnished sunlight
Draws the harshest reaction
from the unsuspecting soul
Brave enough to venture
Mid-afternoon sun
Loathsome breath of new constructions
A rush to blanket
Brown-green earth
Verdant but naive,
Enigmas neath spare canopies.

</div>
