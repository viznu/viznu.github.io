<div class="poem">

Behold the lotus rays of
Our lovely mistakes.
We are blue sky unsullied
by fear's crows.
We are the tempest beating
on defeat's stony shores.

Suffer not
In the dark countries
Of forgetting,
The boats of the moon
Have now slipped past,
To the storms that come
and left us sundered
On these wild shores.

Lost under a sky,
Of stray words,
Fear's shadow.
Stare it down,
Stare it down,
This star's beating heart,
Stare it down.

</div>
