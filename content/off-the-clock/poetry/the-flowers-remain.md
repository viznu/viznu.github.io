<div class="poem">

the flowers remain
when all else is thrown
into high waters......
what waiting may have achieved
light broke into shards.
suffer not
in the dark countries
of forgetting,
the boats of the moon
have now slipped past,
my sweet love
to what storms may come
and left you sundered
On shores
the flowers remain
when all else.....

</div>
